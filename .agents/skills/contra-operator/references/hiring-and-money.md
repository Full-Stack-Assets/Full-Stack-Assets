# Communication, Hiring, Proposals, Invoices, and Money

## Communication

Search/read operations may run automatically. Drafting a message or proposal is `DRAFT`. Sending it is `COMMUNICATION` and requires approval in tiered mode plus any Contra-native confirmation.

## Hiring

Talent search and profile review are `READ`. Shortlists/notes are `DRAFT`. Sending offers, starting engagements, or hiring is `HIRING`; require approval in tiered mode and preserve Contra confirmation/identity gates.

## Financial actions

Creating an invoice draft or payment-plan draft is `DRAFT`. Sending an invoice, accepting financial terms, issuing a refund, initiating payment/payout/withdrawal, or changing billing data is `FINANCIAL` and must respect Contra-native confirmation. Tiered mode requires explicit user approval. Maximum mode never bypasses platform confirmation, payment authentication, identity checks, or fraud/compliance controls.

## Destructive actions

Deleting work, terminating engagements, revoking access, closing accounts, or permanently removing resources is `DESTRUCTIVE`. Require explicit approval even in maximum mode unless the user has made a specific current-turn instruction and the host's safety policy permits execution. Never infer destructive consent from a general autonomy preference.
